## Phase Goal

**As a** buyer, **I want to** open the site (locally, under any base path) and filter, sort, and search a poster grid of seed DVDs, **so that** I can find a movie I want in seconds — backed by one schema-validated catalog file that both the SPA and the ingestion scripts share.

<objective>
Finish the poster card the walking skeleton drew in its thinnest form: rating badge labelled by source, price and condition chips, the placeholder poster for missing/broken images, and the SOLD / Reserved treatments — so a buyer can judge price, condition, rating, and availability from the grid alone (CAT-01, CAT-02, CAT-11, SALE-04). Formatting lives in pure, unit-tested `src/lib/format.ts`.

</objective>
