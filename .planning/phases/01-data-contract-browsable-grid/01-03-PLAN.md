## Phase Goal

**As a** buyer, **I want to** open the site (locally, under any base path) and filter, sort, and search a poster grid of seed DVDs, **so that** I can find a movie I want in seconds — backed by one schema-validated catalog file that both the SPA and the ingestion scripts share.

<objective>
Expand the walking skeleton's search-only slice into the full browse experience: genre/decade/rating/availability filters, four sort orders, result count, Clear all, empty state, and the mobile bottom sheet — all as pure functions in `src/lib/filters.ts` (unit-tested) driven by the URL-state seam plan 01-01 built. A buyer should get from the bare grid to a specific title in under three taps (PROJECT.md success metric).

Purpose: CAT-03..CAT-10 and success criteria 2/3 (filtering half) are the core value of the visitor product; doing them as pure functions keeps the client component thin (D-27) and lets Phase 2 reuse the exact URL contract for "back to grid".
Output: Toolbar, FilterChip(s), FilterSheet, EmptyState components; expanded `filters.ts` + specs; CatalogBrowser wired; GridSkeleton matching the final toolbar.
</objective>
